I am the Chief Scientist at Shenzhen DeepLeap Technology Co., Ltd. My research focuses on reinforcement learning for embodied intelligence.

Previously, I was an Associate Researcher at the Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, and a Postdoctoral Researcher at Peking University. I received my Ph.D. from Zhejiang University, where I also completed my master’s studies in mathematics.

Research interests

My current focus is reinforcement learning for embodied intelligence. My publications also explore the theoretical foundations and optimization methods behind learning and decision-making.

I.

Reinforcement learning

Policy optimization, safe reinforcement learning, and temporal-difference methods.

II.

Embodied intelligence

Reinforcement learning for agents that interact with the physical world.

III.

Learning & optimization

Generalization, sample efficiency, and the foundations of machine learning.
